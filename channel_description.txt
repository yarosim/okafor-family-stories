Okafor Family Stories brings you short, original Nigerian family dramas: mothers and daughters, quiet sacrifices, wedding preparations, and the objects that hold a family together.

Each story is short enough to watch in one sitting, set in contemporary Nigeria, with English dialogue, a little Igbo and Pidgin, and captions you can follow on mute.

Start with "Papa's Machine": a daughter comes home and finds her mother selling her late father's sewing machine, and the truth behind the sale is not what she thinks.

New stories on a steady schedule. Subscribe so you do not miss the next chapter.

All stories are fictional. Characters, voices and scenes are created with AI video tools; any resemblance to real people is coincidental.
